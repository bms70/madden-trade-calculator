Quick Start

  1. Add assets under the team that receives them.
  2. Choose Player or Draft Pick for each asset.
  3. Complete the fields for that asset.
  4. Review the adjusted values and verdict.
  5. Expand Trade Analysis when you want the explanation.
  6. In League mode, select Submit to league once the trade is agreed.

Practice or League

Practice is for trying trades. Nothing is saved.

League is for real trades. Type your league's name and select Enter, then choose Your team and the Other team. The headings and Trade Analysis then show the team names. Your browser remembers the league; Leave league forgets it.

Submitting a Trade

  1. Build the trade with your team on the left and the other team on the right.
  2. Select Submit to league.
  3. Enter your name (and a note if you like), then select Submit.

The trade is logged as Unreviewed until the commissioner checks it. A trade that isn't FAIR can still be submitted; it's flagged for the commissioner.

Build the Trade

Read each side as “What does this team get?” Example: if the User Team sends a quarterback to the CPU Team for two picks, put both picks under User Team Receives and the quarterback under CPU Team Receives.

Each side supports up to six assets in Ruleset v4.4.

Adding a Player

Choose Player. The card will show the player fields. OVR, age, position, and development trait are required before the player contributes value.

Cap hit, years remaining, years in league, current role, and projected role refine the valuation.

Years left must be 1 or more; enter 1 for a contract in its final year.

Rookie note: enter 0 for Years in League to apply Year 0 rookie treatment. Blank is not Year 0.

Adding a Draft Pick

Choose Draft Pick, then select the pick. The Timing list shows only the options that fit that pick:

  • Exact first-round slots (1.01–1.32) are this year's picks, so they are always Current draft.
  • Future 1sts are Next year or Two years away.
  • Round 2–7 picks can be Current draft, Next year, or Two years away.

Optional Fields & Defaults

  • Blank Years in League is neutral and is not Year 0.
  • Blank Years Remaining uses neutral contract control.
  • Either blank role means no role adjustment.
  • Blank Draft Timing means Current draft, except a Future 1st, which means Next year.
  • Blank cap hit is $0M for valuation.

Understanding the Results

Individual Value is one asset’s calculated value. Raw Value is the package total before package-quality adjustments. Adjusted Value is the post-compression package value used in the broader trade evaluation.

Raw Value can look balanced while Adjusted Value shows that one package is stronger.

Using Trade Analysis

Primary Read stays visible. Expand Trade Analysis for Best Asset, Raw Value, Adjusted Value, Draft Capital, Package Structure, and Suggested Correction.

The verdict tells you what the calculator decided; Trade Analysis helps explain why.

Common Trade Types

Player/mixed: the higher eligible positive-value Player determines the protected acquisition side. Standard, Cornerstone, or Elite QB protection sets the directional target.

Exact Player-value tie: protection tier does not break a cross-package tie; the 5% Adjusted Gap path applies.

Draft-only: the stronger pick can trigger 1.15x, 1.08x, 1.04x, or 1.00x protection with the 5% acceptance window.

Common Mistakes

  • Entering assets on the side sending them instead of receiving them.
  • Leaving required player information blank.
  • Leaving Years in League blank when you mean Year 0.
  • Looking only at Raw Value.
  • Assuming more low-value assets automatically fix a trade.
  • Assuming equal draft points automatically mean FAIR.
  • Ignoring Trade Analysis and Suggested Correction.
  • Submitting from the wrong side: your team is always on the left.

Edit or Reset

Select a completed asset card to reopen its inputs. Remove an asset to free a slot. Use Clear trade to reset both sides.

Player Value

Player value considers OVR, age and development, position scarcity, cap cost, contract control, early-career status, lineup role, aging treatment, and QB-to-QB age context.

  • OVR: every point above 60 counts, and each point is worth more than the one before it, so stars gain value faster than depth players. 60 OVR or lower has no value.
  • Position emphasis: QB 1.40x; EDGE/CB/WR 1.20x; OT 1.10x; DT 1.05x; TE/S 1.00x; LB .95x; HB/IOL .90x; FB/K/P .50x.
  • Early career: Year 0 1.50x; Year 1 1.30x; Year 2 1.15x; Year 3+ 1.00x.
  • Contract control: 1/2/3/4/5/6/7 years ≈ 1.00/1.10/1.19/1.27/1.34/1.41/1.47x before cap-tier interaction. Each added year counts a little less than the one before.
  • Aging: players under 80 OVR lose about 10% of value per year from age 30 (31 ≈ .90x, 33 ≈ .73x, 35 ≈ .59x). Players 80+ OVR lose about 8% per year from age 31 (33 ≈ .85x, 35 ≈ .73x, 37 ≈ .62x). Applies to every development trait.

Directional Player Protection

The highest eligible positive-value Player in each package is compared. Same-package exact-value ties use Elite QB > Franchise Cornerstone > Standard, then input order.

  • Elite QB: age ≤26, 90+ OVR, Superstar/X-Factor; 1.30x acquisition requirement.
  • Franchise Cornerstone: age ≤26, 87+ OVR, Superstar/X-Factor; 1.25x scarcity treatment and 1.20x acquisition requirement.
  • Standard player acquisition: 1.10x required return.

FAIR requires the counter-package to fall between the target and target × 1.05, inclusive.

Draft Timing

Delayed draft capital receives less weight: Current draft 1.00x, Next year .85x, Two years away ≈.72x. Exact first-round slots are Current draft only; Future 1sts are Next year or Two years away. Each pick is discounted once.

Package Quality

Quality matters more than quantity. Assets are ranked by individual value on each side. The six available positions receive 100%, 90%, 75%, 60%, 50%, and 50% package credit. Equal values still take sequential positions.

Draft Trade-Ups

  • Acquire 1.01: 1.15x.
  • Acquire 1.02-1.03: 1.08x.
  • Other meaningful trade-up: 1.04x.
  • Near-lateral: 1.00x.

Draft-only FAIR treatment uses a 5% target window.

Verdicts

  • FAIR: inside the FAIR window for the applicable test.
  • QUESTIONABLE: under 5% outside the FAIR window.
  • UNFAIR: 5% to under 15% outside.
  • LEAGUE-BREAKING: 15% or more outside.

Distance is measured from the nearest edge of the window, so falling short and overpaying are judged the same way. For a plain even-trade comparison: FAIR up to a 5% gap, QUESTIONABLE under 10%, UNFAIR under 20%.

Fairness Score (0–100): 100 inside the FAIR window, falling off smoothly the further outside it a trade lands, with the side the trade favors.

League Trades and Review

League trades are logged when submitted and reviewed afterwards by the league's commissioner. Trade History shows each trade's status:

  • Unreviewed: waiting for the commissioner.
  • Reviewed: checked by the commissioner.
  • Corrected: the commissioner fixed the values; the original submission is kept.
  • Pre-league: logged before league mode.

The commissioner can also reverse a trade, with a reason. It leaves Trade History but stays in the audit log. Verdicts are always calculated with the current Ruleset; a review never changes how a trade is valued.

About the Calculator

The calculator uses full internal precision. Display rounding is presentation only. Trade Intelligence includes Primary Read, Best Asset, Raw Value, Adjusted Value, Draft Capital, Package Structure, Fairness Score, and Suggested Correction.
