How to use the calculator. Enter what each team receives, complete the required fields, and use Adjusted Value, the verdict, and Trade Analysis to understand the deal.

Quick Start

  1. Add assets under the team that receives them.
  2. Choose Player or Draft Pick for each asset.
  3. Complete the fields for that asset.
  4. Review the adjusted values and verdict.
  5. Expand Trade Analysis when you want the explanation.

Build the Trade

Read each side as “What does this team get?” Example: if the User Team sends a quarterback to the CPU Team for two picks, put both picks under User Team Receives and the quarterback under CPU Team Receives.

Each side supports up to six assets in Ruleset v4.2.

Adding a Player

Choose Player. The card will show the player fields. OVR, age, position, and development trait are required before the player contributes value.

Cap hit, years remaining, years in league, current role, and projected role refine the valuation.

Rookie note: enter 0 for Years in League to apply Year 0 rookie treatment. Blank is not Year 0.

Adding a Draft Pick

Choose Draft Pick, select the exact current pick or future-first projection, then choose Current draft, Next year, or Two years away.

If timing is blank, the calculator treats it as Current draft.

Optional Fields & Defaults

  • Blank Years in League is neutral and is not Year 0.
  • Blank Years Remaining uses neutral contract control.
  • Either blank role means no role adjustment.
  • Blank Draft Timing means Current draft.
  • Blank cap hit is $0M for valuation.

Understanding the Results

Individual Value is one asset’s calculated value. Raw Value is the package total before package-quality adjustments. Adjusted Value is the post-compression package value used in the broader trade evaluation.

Raw Value can look balanced while Adjusted Value shows that one package is stronger.

Using Trade Analysis

Primary Read stays visible. Expand Trade Analysis for Best Asset, Raw Value, Adjusted Value, Draft Capital, Package Structure, and Suggested Correction.

The verdict tells you what the calculator decided; Trade Analysis helps explain why.

Common Trade Types

Player/mixed: the higher eligible positive-value Player determines the protected acquisition side. Standard, Cornerstone, or Elite QB protection sets the directional target.

Exact Player-value tie: protection tier does not break a cross-package tie; the 8% Adjusted Gap path applies.

Draft-only: the stronger pick can trigger 1.15x, 1.08x, 1.04x, or 1.00x protection with the 8% acceptance window.

Common Mistakes

  • Entering assets on the side sending them instead of receiving them.
  • Leaving required player information blank.
  • Leaving Years in League blank when you mean Year 0.
  • Looking only at Raw Value.
  • Assuming more low-value assets automatically fix a trade.
  • Assuming equal draft points automatically mean FAIR.
  • Ignoring Trade Analysis and Suggested Correction.

Edit or Reset

Select a completed asset card to reopen its inputs. Remove an asset to free a slot. Use Clear trade to reset both sides.

Player Value

Player value considers OVR, age and development, position scarcity, cap cost, contract control, early-career status, lineup role, aging treatment, and QB-to-QB age context.

  • Position emphasis: QB 1.40x; EDGE 1.20x; CB/WR 1.15x; LT 1.10x; DT 1.05x; TE/S 1.00x; LB .95x; HB/IOL .90x; FB/K/P .50x.
  • Early career: Year 0 1.50x; Year 1 1.30x; Year 2 1.15x; Year 3+ 1.00x.
  • Contract control: 1/2/3/4/5+ years = 1.00/1.07/1.16/1.25/1.34x before cap-tier interaction.

Directional Player Protection

The highest eligible positive-value Player in each package is compared. Same-package exact-value ties use Elite QB > Franchise Cornerstone > Standard, then input order.

  • Elite QB: age ≤26, 90+ OVR, Superstar/X-Factor; 1.30x acquisition requirement.
  • Franchise Cornerstone: age ≤26, 87+ OVR, Superstar/X-Factor; 1.25x scarcity treatment and 1.20x acquisition requirement.
  • Standard player acquisition: 1.10x required return.

FAIR requires the counter-package to fall between the target and target × 1.08, inclusive.

Draft Timing

Delayed draft capital receives less weight: Current draft 1.00x, Next year .85x, Two years away .70x. Blank timing defaults to Current.

Package Quality

Quality matters more than quantity. Assets are ranked by individual value on each side. The six available positions receive 100%, 90%, 75%, 60%, 50%, and 50% package credit. Equal values still take sequential positions.

Draft Trade-Ups

  • Acquire 1.01: 1.15x.
  • Acquire 1.02-1.03: 1.08x.
  • Other meaningful trade-up: 1.04x.
  • Near-lateral: 1.00x.

Draft-only FAIR treatment uses an 8% target window.

Verdicts

  • FAIR: applicable acquisition test passes.
  • QUESTIONABLE: not FAIR; adjusted gap under 12%.
  • UNFAIR: 12% to under 22%.
  • LEAGUE-BREAKING: 22% or more.

About the Calculator

The calculator uses full internal precision. Display rounding is presentation only. Trade Intelligence includes Primary Read, Best Asset, Raw Value, Adjusted Value, Draft Capital, Package Structure, and Suggested Correction.

Reference values explain league policy; they are not a reproduction of the complete calculation engine.
